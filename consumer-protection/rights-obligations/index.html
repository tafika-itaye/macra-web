Consumer Rights

Timely and Accurate Bills

The right to accurate and understandable bills for products and services you authorized, and to fair and prompt redress for billing problems.

Quality of Service

The right to receive communications services that meet minimum quality standards as set by MACRA.

Information

The right to clear and accurate information about service terms, pricing, and conditions before and during the service contract.

Redress

The right to lodge a complaint with your service provider and, if unresolved, to escalate the matter to MACRA for independent resolution.

Privacy

The right to have your personal data and communications handled in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Consumer Obligations

Honest Use

Consumers must use communications services lawfully and must not use services to commit fraud, harassment, or any other illegal act.

Payment

Consumers are obligated to pay for services they have used in accordance with agreed terms and conditions.

Accurate Information

Consumers must provide accurate information when subscribing to or applying for communications services.

Reporting

Consumers should report service faults, illegal content, and abuse to their service provider or to MACRA.
