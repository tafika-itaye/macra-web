MACRA is committed to making a positive impact beyond its regulatory mandate. Through consumer education, community broadcasting, and corporate social responsibility, the Authority works to ensure all Malawians benefit from reliable, safe, and affordable communications.

Consumer Education

MACRA conducts outreach campaigns in schools, communities, and through media to educate Malawians about their rights as consumers of communications services, online safety, and responsible digital use.

Community Radio Stations

MACRA supports and licences community radio stations to ensure local and underserved communities have access to information and communications relevant to their needs.

Corporate Social Responsibility

MACRA staff and management participate in community service activities including hospital cleaning exercises, school outreach, and safer internet day campaigns as part of the Authority's commitment to social responsibility.

Online Safety

MACRA leads online safety awareness initiatives in collaboration with schools and community organisations, helping young Malawians navigate the internet safely and responsibly.

Service Charter

MACRA maintains a public service charter that sets out the standards of service the public can expect when interacting with the Authority.
